GATE-10 performs repository-wide independent assessment of physical completeness, identity, metadata, references, dependencies, authority, traceability, manifest integrity, evidence, certification reliability, and release reproducibility. Findings are Critical, Major, Minor, or Informational and include impact, evidence, owner, remediation, due date, and verification. Audit opinion is Conforming, Conforming with Conditions, or Non-Conforming.
