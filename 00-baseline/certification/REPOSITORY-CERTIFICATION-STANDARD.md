Certification determines whether a baseline may be published. Prerequisites are complete upstream baselines, GATE-01 through GATE-08 outcomes, closed critical/major findings, approved exceptions, complete RVEC evidence, and an evidence-based review. Certification records scope, artifact set, gate outcomes, findings, conditions, authority, effective period, and revocation criteria. GATE-09 verifies the certification itself.

## Document control

This controlled document is immutable in this release. Changes require the approved ADR and change-control process. Cross-references use Constitutional Document IDs; filenames are non-authoritative.
