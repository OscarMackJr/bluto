An exception request identifies control, reason, risk, scope, compensating controls, owner, approver, start/end dates, monitoring, and remediation. Exceptions cannot waive the authoritative Identity Spine, permit raw strong identifiers at rest, authorize implicit cross-tenant resolution, or grant AI architecture authority. Expired exceptions fail closed.
