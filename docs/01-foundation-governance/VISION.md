Bluto will provide a small, auditable enterprise identity spine that assigns stable Party identifiers, maps source records deterministically, preserves lineage and historical truth, and exposes governed mappings before downstream analysis. It will not become a customer profile, master-data hub, or query-time matching engine.

Success means correct identity before coverage, tenant-safe resolution, no raw strong identifiers at rest, explainable rules, controlled stewardship, and sufficient architectural precision for autonomous implementation.
